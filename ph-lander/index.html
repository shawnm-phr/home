Six Manual HR Processes You No Longer Have To Run By Hand

1. One Employee Record. Every Site.

Replaces: Separate employee spreadsheets across plants, branches and accounts.

Personal, professional and statutory information sits in one central record, with custom fields where required.

2. Attendance Flows Straight Into Payroll.

Replaces: Downloading attendance, reconciling it and retyping overtime, lateness and early departures.

Attendance data feeds payroll directly, removing another manual step from every cut-off.

3. Overtime, Shifts and Holiday Rules Apply Automatically.

Replaces: Working out different multipliers and shift rules by hand.

Set shift schedules, grace periods and overtime rules once and apply them consistently.

4. Payroll Errors Are Flagged Before Approval.

Replaces: Finding a calculation error after employees have already been paid.

The system checks payroll calculations before finalisation, while approval controls prevent changes after sign-off.

5. See Attendance Across Every Site Live.

Replaces: Waiting for manually prepared attendance reports.

See who is present, late or absent across locations, teams and departments from one view.

6. Employees Handle Routine HR Tasks Themselves.

Replaces: Payslip, leave, policy and attendance requests constantly coming back to HR.

Employees can access everyday HR services through PeoplesHR mobile self-service.
