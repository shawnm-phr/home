What We'll Cover

PeoplesHR v10.3 brings HR closer to a single vision: work that runs through intelligence and conversation. In this webinar, we'll show you Lexi AI Insights' smarter engine, now live on mobile, and introduce the new Lexi Super Agent, a single AI agent that handles HR, payroll, and time requests in one conversation. We'll also walk through Work From Anywhere with configurable geofencing, the new Payroll Preprocessing Workflow for catching payroll errors before they run, faster off-cycle payslip access, digital signing straight from Report Navigator, and the latest compliance updates for Sri Lanka, the Philippines, and Indonesia.

  • Lexi AI Insights: now smarter, and available on mobile
  • Lexi Super Agent: one agent for HR, payroll, and time requests
  • Work From Anywhere: flexible arrangements with location control
  • Payroll Preprocessing Workflow: catch and fix payroll errors before they run
  • Faster payslip access, plus the latest compliance updates for Sri Lanka, the Philippines, and Indonesia
