Attendance. Access. Security. In One Connected System.

PeoplesHR Tracking isn't just about clocking in and out. It's a complete workforce tracking and security layer built for organizations that need accurate attendance data, controlled facility access, and a single source of truth that flows straight into payroll.

Hardware Built for Accuracy, Not Just Attendance

Every PeoplesHR Tracking deployment is backed by rugged, reliable devices designed to eliminate time theft and give employees a fast, frictionless way to check in — on the factory floor, at the front desk, or at a secure entry point.

Fingerprint Machines

Our fingerprint machines use high-precision sensors to verify identity in under a second, eliminating buddy punching and manual sign-in sheets. Each fingerprint scanner is built for high-traffic environments and continues to work reliably even with worn or rough fingerprints — a common failure point in cheaper hardware. Every unit is sourced directly through our authorized ZKTeco partnership, so you get genuine hardware backed by a real manufacturer warranty.

Face Recognition Kiosks

For a completely hands-free check-in, our face recognition devices identify employees in real time as they approach — no card to tap, no button to press. It's faster at shift-change rush hours and removes the hygiene concerns of shared touch surfaces.

Self-Service Kiosk Machines

Beyond clocking in, our kiosk machines let employees view their schedule, request leave, and check their timecard right at the device — cutting down on HR queries and giving your team more control over their own attendance record.

Card & PIN Readers

For sites that prefer badge-based access, our card and PIN terminals integrate with the same backend, so every method of clocking in — biometric or otherwise — feeds into one unified attendance record.

An Authorized ZKTeco Partner — Not a Grey-Market Reseller

Every fingerprint machine, face recognition kiosk, and access control device we supply comes through our official ZKTeco partnership. That matters more than it sounds — devices bought through unauthorized channels often carry voided warranties, no access to official firmware or security updates, and no accountable support line back to the manufacturer.

  • Genuine hardware, guaranteed — no counterfeit or grey-market units
  • Full manufacturer-backed warranty on every device
  • Certified installation and technical support
  • Direct access to official firmware and security updates

Control Who Goes Where — and Know It Instantly

PeoplesHR Tracking extends beyond time capture into physical security. The same devices and platform that track attendance can also manage access to restricted zones, server rooms, warehouses, or entire buildings.

  • Role-based and shift-based access permissions
  • Door, gate, and turnstile integration
  • Real-time alerts for unauthorized access attempts
  • Full audit trail for compliance and investigations
  • Centralized management across multiple sites and buildings

Built to Work Seamlessly With PeoplesHR Time and Pay

Attendance data is only as useful as what it feeds into. PeoplesHR Tracking connects directly with PeoplesHR Time and Pay, so every punch, exception, and access event flows straight through to pay calculation — no exports, no re-keying, no reconciliation headaches at month-end.

Automatic Hours & Overtime

Worked hours, late arrivals, early departures, and overtime are calculated automatically from device data.

Exception Handling

Missed punches, shift mismatches, and unusual access patterns are flagged for review before payroll runs.

Audit-Ready Records

Every attendance and access event is timestamped and stored, giving you a defensible record for audits and disputes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of devices does PeoplesHR Tracking support?

PeoplesHR Tracking works with fingerprint machines, face recognition kiosks, card readers, and PIN terminals, so you can choose the check-in method that fits each site — or mix methods across locations.

Is PeoplesHR Tracking an authorized ZKTeco partner?

Yes. PeoplesHR Tracking is a fully authorized ZKTeco partner, which means every fingerprint machine, face recognition kiosk, and access control device we supply is genuine, backed by manufacturer warranty, and eligible for official firmware and security updates. Buying through an unauthorized reseller can mean voided warranties, counterfeit hardware, and no direct support line back to the manufacturer — risks you avoid by working with an authorized partner.

Can a single kiosk handle both attendance and access control?

Yes. The same kiosk machine used for clocking in can also manage access to a restricted door or zone, so you're not installing separate hardware for each function.

Does attendance data flow automatically into payroll?

Yes. PeoplesHR Tracking connects directly with PeoplesHR Time and Pay, so hours, overtime, and exceptions are calculated automatically without manual data entry.

Can PeoplesHR Tracking be deployed across multiple branches?

Yes. Devices at every site sync back to a single central platform, so you get one consolidated view of attendance and access across your entire organization.
