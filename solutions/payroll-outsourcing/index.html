Risk & Compliance

Sensitive Payroll Data Deserves More Than Basic Processing.

Payroll contains some of an organization's most sensitive information, including salaries, bank details, identification numbers, tax records and employee benefits. Our payroll operations combine defined responsibilities, controlled processing and secure technology to help reduce operational, compliance and data-handling risk.

Processing Within an Agreed Scope

Payroll information is processed according to documented client instructions, approved payroll rules and defined responsibilities.

Controlled Access

Payroll access is restricted according to authorized roles and operational requirements.

Confidentiality

Personnel involved in payroll processing are subject to confidentiality and information-handling obligations.

Accuracy and Validation

Defined review points, reconciliation checks and exception reporting help improve payroll-data accuracy.

Data Retention and Disposal

Payroll data is retained, returned or securely disposed of according to contractual, operational and legal requirements.

Audit and Traceability

Payroll records, reports and approvals are maintained to support internal reviews, external audits and compliance requirements.

Incident Management

Structured escalation and incident-management procedures support timely investigation and response where required.

Compliant with Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act

PeoplesHR Payroll Outsourcing operates in compliance with Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act and applies appropriate organizational and technical controls when processing personal data on behalf of clients. Our approach addresses key processor responsibilities, including:

  • Processing data according to documented instructions
  • Maintaining confidentiality
  • Supporting authorized compliance reviews
  • Restricting access to personal data
  • Managing data retention, return and deletion
  • Supporting incident investigation and response
  • Applying controls to third parties and subprocessors where applicable

Sri Lanka's Data Protection Authority states that the main provisions of the Act became operational on 18 March 2025. Sri Lanka Data Protection Authority

Frequently Asked Questions

What is payroll outsourcing?

Payroll outsourcing means appointing a specialist provider to manage agreed payroll activities on your behalf. These can include data validation, payroll calculation, statutory processing, payslips, bank files, reporting and employee-query support.

Will we lose control of payroll?

No. Your organization retains ownership of payroll policies, employee-data decisions and final payroll approval. PeoplesHR manages the agreed operational process and provides the reports required for informed approval.

Can PeoplesHR work with our existing HR or attendance systems?

Yes. The delivery model can be designed around PeoplesHR or agreed data inputs from other systems. Integration requirements are assessed during the discovery stage.

Can you handle multiple companies or locations?

Yes. Payroll can be structured for multiple legal entities, branches, locations, departments and cost centres, subject to the agreed country and service scope.

Does PeoplesHR handle statutory filings and payments?

Statutory calculations and reporting outputs can be included. Filing, remittance and representation responsibilities are confirmed separately for each country and engagement.

How is payroll accuracy maintained?

We use structured input checks, exception reporting, payroll comparisons, reconciliations and client approval checkpoints before finalization.

How is employee payroll data protected?

Payroll data is managed through controlled access, confidentiality requirements, secure technology, defined retention practices and documented processing procedures.

Can PeoplesHR support employee payroll queries?

Yes. Employee payroll-query support can be added to the outsourcing package with defined channels, response responsibilities and escalation procedures.

How long does payroll outsourcing implementation take?

The timeline depends on workforce size, payroll complexity, data quality, number of entities and integration requirements. A transition plan is prepared after the initial payroll assessment.
